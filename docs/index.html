Why KnowIt?

🎯

Two Question Types

Alternates between Multiple Choice and Type-In questions so you can't just guess.

🔥

Streak Bonuses

Get on a roll and earn +5 bonus points for every consecutive correct answer.

✨

Pure Compose Animations

Card shake, green glow, confetti rain, animated score counter — no third-party libs.

🏆

High Score Tracking

Your best score is saved between sessions. Can you hit the max 295 points?

📊

Letter Grades

Results screen scores you from A+ to F based on accuracy and total points.

📱

Offline & Lightweight

No internet required, no ads, no account needed. Just open and play.

5 Categories

10 pairs of questions — 4 per category — covering a broad range of knowledge.

🔬 Science
📜 History
🌍 Geography
🎬 Pop Culture
💻 Tech

How Scoring Works

EventPoints
Correct answer+10 pts
Streak bonus (2nd+ consecutive correct)+5 pts extra
Wrong answer0 pts, streak resets
Maximum possible score (20/20 + full streaks)295 pts
